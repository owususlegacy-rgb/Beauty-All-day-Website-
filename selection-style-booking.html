Booking fee & refund policy

Booking fees are non-refundable, except if the salon is unable to honour your confirmed appointment because of a scheduling error on our part, such as overbooking. In that case your R50 fee is refunded in full.

Need to cancel or reschedule? We'll move your booking to a new date at no extra cost. Your booking fee carries over, so you won't pay again.
