Terms & conditions

The short version: your booking fee holds your slot, the salon settles the service cost, and we'll always try to put things right if something goes wrong on our side.

Who this applies to

These terms apply to anyone booking an appointment through this website for any of the salons listed on it, located at Boulders Shopping Centre and China Town Midrand Mall, Midrand, South Africa. By completing a booking you agree to the terms below.

Booking fees

Each service you select carries a R50 booking fee, paid online when you book. This fee secures your appointment slot. It is separate from, and does not cover, the cost of the service itself. That amount is paid directly to the salon on the day of your appointment.

Services are delivered by the individual salon you book with, and each salon sets its own services and prices.

Payment

Booking fees are processed securely through Yoco. We do not see or store your card details: they are handled entirely by Yoco's payment platform. After your payment succeeds you are returned to this site and prompted to confirm your booking details over WhatsApp.

Cancellations and rescheduling

Booking fees are non-refundable, with one exception: if the salon is unable to honour your confirmed appointment because of a scheduling error on our part (for example, overbooking), your booking fee will be refunded in full.

If you need to cancel, or if we need to move your appointment for any other reason, we will reschedule you to a new date and time at no additional cost. Your original booking fee carries over, so you won't be charged again. Please tell us as early as you can on WhatsApp.

Arriving for your appointment

Please arrive on time. If you are running late, message us on WhatsApp so we can let you know whether the salon can still accommodate you. Repeated no-shows without notice may affect your ability to book online in future.

Accuracy of information

You are responsible for giving accurate contact details (name and phone number) when booking, so we can reach you to confirm or discuss your appointment. We are not responsible for missed confirmations caused by incorrect details.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as our booking process changes. The version in effect at the time of your booking applies to that booking.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent to us on WhatsApp: 068 096 5235.
