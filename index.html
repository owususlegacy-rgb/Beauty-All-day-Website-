Booking

Book in three steps

1

Choose your services

Pick a salon, select the services you want, then a day and time that suit you.

2

Pay the R50 booking fee

A R50 fee per service holds your slot. You pay securely through Yoco, so your card details never touch this site.

3

Confirm on WhatsApp

Once payment goes through, WhatsApp opens with your booking ready to send, so the salon knows to expect you.

Good to know

The booking fee is separate from the price of your service, which you pay at the salon on the day.

Booking fees are non-refundable, except if we can't honour your confirmed slot because of a scheduling error on our side, in which case you're refunded in full. Need to change your time? We'll reschedule you at no extra cost. Read the full terms
